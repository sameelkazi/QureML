Core Scientific Claim & Research Question

QureML investigates whether parameterized quantum circuits provide genuine clinical utility in disease triage when evaluated under identical parameter counts, isolating quantum computational contributions across 6 real biomedical benchmarks with rigorous statistical corrections.

balance

Honest Headline Finding: Statistical Parity with Classical Baselines

Across six real clinical datasets, the 6-qubit hybrid quantum classifier (Control A, 73 parameters) achieves statistical parity with a strictly parameter-matched classical neural network (Control B, 73 parameters) under Holm-Bonferroni family-wise error rate control (m=3, α=0.05). Rather than making exaggerated claims of quantum supremacy on low-dimensional tabular data, QureML reports scientific truth with transparency.

1. Statistical Parity
WDBC, Heart, Liver, CKD

Both models operate near ceiling; quantum and classical representations are equivalent in discriminative power.

2. Confirmatory A > D
Parkinson's (p = 0.00052)

Variational training significantly beats random fixed quantum weights, confirming the quantum layer learns real biomedical features.

3. Real Hardware Wins
Bandwidth & Uncertainty

292 B/round federated payload (~2,000× lighter than ResNet-18) and shot-based quantum uncertainty quantification.
