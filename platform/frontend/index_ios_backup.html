Wisconsin Diagnostic Breast Cancer (569 Patients)

Evaluated across the 114-patient locked test cohort. Achieved 98.25% accuracy, 0.9983 ROC-AUC, zero false negatives at τ=0.10 operating threshold, and confirmed statistical parity (p=1.0000) against parameter-matched classical neural networks.

Stress-tested in ultra-scarce clinical regimes across 25 independent random seeds. Demonstrated statistical significance (p=0.0005) in small sample scaling where parameter compactness prevents severe overfitting.
