Translational Milestones & R&D Challenges

Stated honestly because credibility matters more than an illusion of completeness

science

1. Hard Quantum Advantage

Statistical parity with a parameter-matched classical MLP across 6 datasets is the honest reality. Demonstrating true computational advantage requires structured biomedical feature spaces that are provably classically intractable to simulate.

memory

2. QPU Noise Characterization

Primary benchmarks use PennyLane statevector simulation. Zero-noise extrapolation (ZNE) and randomized benchmarking on physical multi-qubit QPUs (IBM Heron) will be expanded beyond free-tier constraints.

verified

3. Prospective Clinical Trials

Evaluated strictly on public, de-identified retrospective cohorts (WDBC, Cleveland Heart). Formal IRB-approved hospital trials in Indian tertiary cancer centers are required before any clinical diagnostic deployment.

hub

4. Multi-Hospital Federated Network

The 292-byte/round federated learning result is verified in simulation. The next phase will deploy live differential privacy (DP-FedAvg) nodes across real inter-hospital TLS networks.

scale

5. High-Dimensional Quantum Ansätze

Currently uses 6 principal components to ensure noise-resilient execution on NISQ devices. Higher-dimensional quantum convolutional layers (Quanv) for gigapixel histopathology slides are planned.

gavel

6. CDSCO / FDA SaMD Certification

Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) regulatory compliance pathway under ISO 13485, IEC 62304, and Good Clinical Practice guidelines will be formally initiated in post-competition development.
